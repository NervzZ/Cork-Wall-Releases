Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

Cork Wall ("the App") is a digital pinboard for small communities to share event notes and other pinned items. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices about it.

Who runs the App

Cork Wall is operated by Alexandre Mohsen as a non-commercial side project. Contact: julien.jordan@epfl.ch.

What we collect

The App stores the minimum data needed for it to function:

  • Anonymous account identifier. When you first open the App, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous user ID. This ID is not tied to your name, email, phone number, or any other personal detail unless you choose to provide them.
  • Profile information you enter. A display name (which can be a nickname) and your preferred language.
  • Content you create. Walls you create or join, pin notes (titles, descriptions, dates), RSVPs, and read-state markers indicating which pins you have opened.
  • Camera access (only when you use it). If you scan a QR code to join a shared wall, the camera reads the code locally on your device. No photos or video are stored or transmitted.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or device analytics. The App contains no third-party advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking technology.

How we use your data

  • To let you create and join shared walls.
  • To display pin notes, RSVPs, and read-state to other members of the same wall.
  • To enforce access controls (for example, hiding pins flagged as confidential from members not on the allowlist).

Who can see your data

Content you post on a wall is visible to other members of that same wall. If you mark a pin confidential and pick a viewer list, only the listed members and wall admins can see its content. The App's developer does not actively review or sell your data, and we do not share data with third parties for advertising or analytics.

Data is stored on Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Google's handling of that data is governed by their own terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Data retention and deletion

  • Pin notes and walls remain stored until they are explicitly deleted by the creator or a wall admin.
  • You can leave any wall at any time from the wall's member list, which removes your membership and your RSVP / read-state markers for that wall.
  • To delete your anonymous account and all associated data, contact us at julien.jordan@epfl.ch with the request and we will erase the records linked to your account ID.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, write to julien.jordan@epfl.ch.
